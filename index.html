MatchTrack

Game, training and load-monitoring reports for football teams, on macOS

Turn a GPS tracker export into a report your coaches will actually read.

Load the CSV your tracking system produces, and the app builds game, training and load-monitoring reports with your club's branding — ready to export as PDF or CSV. Everything happens on your own Mac.
