MatchTrack — Support

Game, training and load-monitoring reports from GPS tracker exports, on Mac and iPad

Need help? Email matchstatssupport@gmail.com

Tell us whether you are using the Mac or the iPad app, your macOS or iPadOS version, and what you were doing when it went wrong. If it is about a particular report, the session date and the report type — game, training or load monitoring — help us find it. If a CSV will not load, saying which GPS system it came from is the single most useful detail.

We may ask for a support package. Make one with Generate Support Package at the bottom of the subscription screen, which the subscription button at the top of the window opens. On a Mac it is saved to your Downloads folder; on an iPad it goes to Files › On My iPad › MatchTrack › Support and the share sheet opens. Attach it to your email. It is never sent on its own, and what it contains is listed in the privacy policy.

Does it need an internet connection?

Not to build reports. They are built on your own Mac or iPad and nothing is uploaded. The app does check your subscription with Apple through the App Store — when it opens, when you come back to it, and from time to time while it runs — and taking out or restoring a subscription needs a connection.

Where are my reports kept?

On a Mac, in the app's own folder in your Library, ~/Library/Containers/com.thomasgrech.matchtrack, organised per workspace. Reports you export as PDF or CSV, backup files and support packages normally go to your Downloads folder.

On an iPad, inside the app. Everything you export appears in Files › On My iPad › MatchTrack, in folders called Exports, Backups and Support, and the share sheet opens each time so you can AirDrop, mail, print or save it somewhere else. That folder is deleted along with the app, so keep a backup file you care about somewhere else too.

The app's data is included whenever you back up the device — with Time Machine on a Mac, or an iCloud or computer backup of an iPad, where the app leaves its automatic restore points out. MatchTrack keeps no copy of its own anywhere else, so a lost Mac or iPad with no backup means lost reports. A backup file made in the app, kept somewhere other than that device, is the simplest insurance.

If you delete a report or change something by mistake, the backup window — the cloud button at the top of Report Settings — also lists automatic restore points the app keeps on the device for the open workspace, and can put its reports and settings back as they were. Photographs and badges are not in restore points. A workspace you delete cannot be brought back from them, so make a backup file before you delete one.

Loading a CSV on an iPad

Tap Choose a GPS CSV and pick the export in the Files picker, from iCloud Drive or On My iPad, or drag one in from Files. You can also open a CSV in MatchTrack straight from Mail, Files or AirDrop; if a session of your own is on screen when you do, the app asks before it replaces it.

My CSV will not load

The app reads the CSV exports produced by GPS tracking systems, which differ between vendors and between firmware versions. If yours is rejected, send us the file, or a copy with every player row deleted so that only the column headings are left (some exports start with a few lines of session details above them — keep those too), and we will tell you whether it is supported.

Check first that you are exporting the per-player summary rather than the raw position log.

The subscription options are blank

That means the App Store did not return the prices, usually a temporary network problem. Use Try again on the paywall, and if it persists, check that you are signed in to the App Store and try again in a few minutes. On a Mac, hovering over the message shows the underlying reason, which is worth including if you email us.

I already subscribed but the app is locked

Use Restore Purchases on the subscription screen. That asks Apple to re-check your Apple Account, which is also what to do after reinstalling, moving to a new Mac, or starting on an iPad. One subscription covers MatchTrack on both Mac and iPad, as long as both use the same Apple Account.

How do I cancel?

Manage Subscription on the subscription screen opens Apple's own subscription settings — on a Mac in the App Store, on an iPad in a sheet over the app — where you can change or cancel. Cancelling stops the renewal; the subscription keeps working until the period you have paid for ends.

To ask for a refund, use Request a Refund on the same screen, which appears once you have a plan. It opens Apple's own refund request; Apple decides refunds, not us.

Moving to a new Mac

Install the app, then use Restore Purchases. Your reports do not transfer on their own — copy them across with Migration Assistant, or from a Time Machine backup, or make a backup file on the old Mac and restore it on the new one as described below, before you retire the old Mac.

Moving between a Mac and an iPad

There is no automatic sync: a change on one device does not appear on the other. Your work moves in a backup file that you make on one and restore on the other. From a Mac to an iPad:

  1. On the Mac, open Report Settings, click the cloud button at the top, and choose Save… beside All workspaces (or This workspace). A .gpsbackup file is saved to your Downloads folder.
  2. Get it to the iPad — AirDrop it, put it in iCloud Drive, or email it to yourself.
  3. On the iPad, install MatchTrack and sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account. If the app is locked, use Restore Purchases: restoring a backup needs an active subscription. If the first-run setup window is showing, choose Skip for now — the app opens a file only when no other window is in the way.
  4. Open the backup file in MatchTrack from the AirDrop prompt, Files or Mail. Or, in the app, use Restore a backup in the empty session list, or Choose a backup file in the backup window.
  5. The app asks before it restores, and then reloads. Opened from AirDrop, Files or Mail, a backup keeps both copies of any workspace you already have: the backup's copy is added beside yours, marked (imported). To skip that workspace or replace yours with the backup instead, open the backup window, set If a workspace already exists, and then use Choose a backup file.

The other way round works the same: on the iPad, make the backup from the backup window, send it to the Mac with the share sheet, and on the Mac use Choose a backup file in the backup window.

A backup file holds your reports, GPS files, player photographs, badges and settings, so look after it as you would the reports themselves.

Privacy

Nothing you load into the app is sent to us. It leaves your Mac or iPad only in files you export or share yourself, and in your device's own backups if you use them. The details are in the privacy policy.
