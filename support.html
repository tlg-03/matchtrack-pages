GPS Report Builder — Support

Game, training and load-monitoring reports from GPS tracker exports

Need help? Email matchstatssupport@gmail.com

Tell us your macOS version and what you were doing when it went wrong. If it is about a particular report, the session date and the report type — game, training or load monitoring — help us find it. If a CSV will not load, saying which GPS system it came from is the single most useful detail.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Reports are built on your own Mac and nothing is uploaded. You can work at a ground with no signal at all. A connection is needed only to take out or restore a subscription, which Apple handles.

Where are my reports kept?

In the app's own folder in your Library, organised per workspace. Reports you export as PDF or CSV go to your Downloads folder.

They are ordinary files, so they are included whenever you back up your Mac — with Time Machine, for example. There is no cloud copy, so a lost Mac with no backup means lost reports.

My CSV will not load

The app reads the CSV exports produced by GPS tracking systems, which differ between vendors and between firmware versions. If yours is rejected, send us the file — or just its first two lines, which carry the column headings — and we will tell you whether it is supported.

Check first that you are exporting the per-player summary rather than the raw position log.

The subscription options are blank

That means the App Store did not return the prices, usually a temporary network problem. Use Try again on the paywall, and if it persists, check that you are signed in to the App Store and try again in a few minutes. Hovering the message shows the underlying reason, which is worth including if you email us.

I already subscribed but the app is locked

Use Restore Purchases on the subscription screen. That asks Apple to re-check your Apple Account, which is also what to do after reinstalling or moving to a new Mac.

How do I cancel?

Manage Subscription on the subscription screen opens the App Store's own subscription settings, where you can change or cancel. Cancelling stops the renewal; the subscription keeps working until the period you have paid for ends.

Moving to a new Mac

Install the app, then use Restore Purchases. Your reports do not transfer on their own — copy them across with Migration Assistant, or from a Time Machine backup, before you retire the old Mac.

Privacy

Nothing you load into the app leaves your Mac. The details are in the privacy policy.
